## 3. Detailed Long Description (300–400 words)

Transform your Chess.com experience with **PawnHub**, the all-in-one cosmetic customizer that brings custom chess board themes, handcrafted vector piece sets, meme audio sound effects, and animated game reactions to your board. Whether you want to play with glowing cyberpunk neon boards, mold your own photos into distinct chess piece silhouettes, or hear hilarious sound reactions on every capture and checkmate, PawnHub gives you complete creative freedom.

### Why Chess Players Love PawnHub

- **5 Handcrafted Vector Piece Sets**: Upgrade from standard pieces to 5 original vector sets—including *Crystal Glass*, *Neon Wireframe*, *Gold & Obsidian*, *Paper Cut*, and *Friendly Monsters*—each engineered with distinct silhouettes so knights, bishops, queens, and kings remain instantly recognizable at blitz speed.
- **Molded Silhouette Studio**: Upload any custom image or photo and mold it cleanly into the exact silhouette of any chess piece with dual-contrast light and dark rims that pop against any square. Frame, zoom, and position your photo non-destructively in real time.
- **12 Immersive Board Themes**: Switch effortlessly between 12 vibrant themes like *Cyberpunk Neon*, *Candy Land*, *Lava Volcano*, *Deep Space*, and *Haunted Mansion*, with matching checkerboard palettes.
- **High-Impact Comedy Mode**: Experience dramatic "Bonk & Fly" capture animations, comic shockwave text bursts, floating RIP markers, and King panic reactions. Commentary speech bubbles display lighthearted, original jokes after captures, checks, and game-ending moves.
- **Custom Soundpack Studio**: Assign custom audio to 12 distinct game events (moves, captures, checks, promotions, and checkmates). Extract audio directly from video clips (MP4/WebM), trim clips with an interactive waveform editor, apply smooth fades, and export or share your custom soundpacks.
- **100% Private & Local**: Zero data collection, zero network requests, and zero tracking. All custom images and sound files stay securely on your device.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Does PawnHub affect my game moves, clocks, or ratings?**  
No. PawnHub is strictly cosmetic. It never alters chess moves, engine evaluation, clocks, or game logic.

**Do my uploaded photos or sound files get sent to a server?**  
Never. All custom pieces and sounds are processed in-browser and stored exclusively in your local browser storage (`chrome.storage.local`).

**Can I use PawnHub on all Chess.com game modes?**  
Yes. PawnHub operates seamlessly on Play vs Computer, Live Blitz/Bullet/Rapid, Daily Chess, Puzzles, and Analysis boards.
