07 / Privacy Commitment

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

PawnHub is an open-source, cosmetic-only browser extension for Chess.com. We believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. This Privacy Policy details our strict commitment to zero data collection.

1. Zero Data Collection

  • No Personal Information: PawnHub does not collect, track, log, transmit, or sell any personal data, identifiers, IP addresses, browsing history, or gameplay data.
  • No Analytics or Trackers: PawnHub contains zero third-party telemetry, analytics services, cookies, tracking pixels, or external scripts.
  • No External Network Traffic: PawnHub makes zero outbound network requests. All functionality executes strictly inside your local browser environment.

2. Local Storage & User Uploads

  • Device-Only Storage: All user settings, chosen themes, uploaded piece photos, and custom audio files are saved exclusively in your browser's local sandbox using the chrome.storage.local API.
  • Uploaded Content Remains Private: Any images or video/audio clips you upload for piece molding or sound effects are processed entirely on your computer via HTML5 Canvas and the Web Audio API. They never leave your device and are never transmitted to any cloud server or third party.

3. Browser Extension Permissions

When running as a browser extension, PawnHub requests only the permissions strictly required to function locally:

Permission Purpose & Technical Justification
storage Required to save user preferences, selected board themes, comedy toggles, custom piece cutouts, and sound assignments locally on the device.
unlimitedStorage Required to store user-uploaded custom piece images and high-fidelity sound clips locally without hitting the default 5MB storage quota.
https://www.chess.com/*
https://chess.com/*		 Required to detect chessboard DOM elements on Chess.com pages in order to apply cosmetic styles, piece replacements, and visual/audio reactions.

4. Fair Play & Compliance

PawnHub operates strictly as a client-side visual overlay. It does not interface with chess engines, analyze board positions, calculate moves, or provide gameplay recommendations. It complies fully with Chess.com's fair play standards.

5. Open Source Verification

Because PawnHub is licensed under the MIT License, our entire codebase is open for public audit. You can inspect every line of code on GitHub.
