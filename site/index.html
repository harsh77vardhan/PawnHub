05 / Silhouette Studio

Mold any photo into a chess piece.

Never lose track of piece types when using custom avatars. PawnHub automatically clips your photos into handcrafted chess silhouettes and adds high-contrast side rims.

  • Silhouette Masking: Clips images into bold, unmistakable chess profiles.
  • Contrasting Rims: Light rim for White, dark rim for Black so pieces remain visible on any square.
  • Optional Badges: Display subtle corner role letters (P, N, B, R, Q, K) for blitz clarity.
  • 100% Client-Side: Processed locally with HTML5 Canvas. Your photos never leave your machine.

08 / Privacy Commitment

Your data never leaves your device.

PawnHub is designed from the ground up as a strictly offline, client-side cosmetic extension.

Zero Telemetry

No Google Analytics, no tracking pixels, and no third-party scripts. We never log user counts, game histories, or sessions.

Local Storage Only

All theme choices, soundpack assignments, and cropped piece photos stay in chrome.storage.local on your own machine.

Strictly Cosmetic

No engine assistance, no move suggestions, and no cheating. PawnHub only alters local visual presentation and sound synthesis.
